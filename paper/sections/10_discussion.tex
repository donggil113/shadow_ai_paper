\section{Negative results, limitations and outlook}
\label{sec:discussion}

\paragraph{What did not survive.} Three claims made while this work was in
progress were refuted by our own checks and are recorded rather than removed.
(i) \emph{The midpoint ordering is minimax-optimal for AUROC in general.}
False: exhaustive search finds counterexamples with unequal widths
(\cref{app:audit-ranking}); the corrected claim is \cref{prop:midrank}
(equal widths), and the recommendation stands because measured gains
elsewhere are negligible. (ii) \emph{The identified box with cross-fitted
nuisances is a per-unit confidence set.} False: it covers $p(x)$ for about
half of the units (\cref{tab:nuisance}); the corrected claim is
\cref{thm:nuisance}. (iii) \emph{COMPAS provides ground truth.} Overstated:
detention incapacitates, and holding time at risk fixed raises the odds ratio
from $\nCompasTarORRecorded$ to $\nCompasTarORExposure$
(\cref{app:compas-audit}); the corrected wording is \emph{recorded outcome}
and both anchors are reported.

\paragraph{Limitations.} $\dcsm(\Gamma)$ bounds the \emph{worst} unit and
permits every unit to be maximally tilted at once; the budgeted
(\cref{rem:budget}) and directional (\cref{rem:directional}) variants relax
this at the price of more structure from the analyst. \Cref{prop:falsify}
needs random case assignment. The bounds inherit whatever error is in
$\hat e$ and $\hat p_1$; \cref{thm:nuisance} makes the cost explicit, and the
binned intervals it licenses are conservative. The medical arm of this paper is specified but not
yet run: the credentialed MIMIC-IV-ECG and ECHO files were not reachable from
the environment in which the manuscript was produced, and we report no medical
number in the main text rather than a simulated one.

\paragraph{Choosing $\Gamma$} cannot be avoided (\cref{thm:impossible}), so
it should be done in the open: a lower bound from leniency variation
(\cref{prop:falsify}), an anchor from any setting where the censored outcomes
were recorded anyway (on COMPAS, $\nCompasTarORRecorded$ recorded and
$\nCompasTarORExposure$ with exposure held fixed), and a break-even report of
the $\Gamma$ at which each conclusion flips.

\paragraph{Outlook.} The rank identity linearises AUROC under \emph{any}
constraint set, so sharp AUROC bounds should follow for covariate shift, label
noise and fairness-constrained classes.\label{lastmainpage}
